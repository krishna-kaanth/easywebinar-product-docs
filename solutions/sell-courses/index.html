Course creators selling through webinars

Review Course creators selling through webinars: Automated webinars deliver a recorded presentation through a repeatable registration and attendance workflow.

Full Markdown
Home / solutions / sell courses

Overview

This workflow is for course creators evaluating a course launch and repeatable course-selling presentation. The aim is course enrolment, using supported webinar delivery, attendee interaction and the appropriate next-action path.

The requirements below are the complete 35-item buyer set from the supplied inventory. They describe what this audience looks for, including needs that are unconfirmed or unavailable. Do not read an aspirational requirement title as a guaranteed business result.

A practical workflow for this buyer

Prepare a lesson-and-offer presentation, register a test prospect and trace the path from watching to the approved course checkout.

Begin with mandatory constraints: the event format, intended audience, required next action, existing stack and budget. Validate those before adding optional AI, CRM or multistream modules. Use the linked shared capability pages to inspect the actual product evidence.
