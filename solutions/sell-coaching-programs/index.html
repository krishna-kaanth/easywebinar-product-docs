Coaches and consultants selling programs or consultations

Review Coaches and consultants selling programs or consultations: Booking-tool connections let a webinar audience proceed to an appointment or consultation.

Full Markdown
Home / solutions / sell coaching programs

Overview

This workflow is for coaches and consultants evaluating a coaching programme, paid workshop or consultation funnel. The aim is a qualified consultation or programme purchase, using supported webinar delivery, attendee interaction and the appropriate next-action path.

The requirements below are the complete 30-item buyer set from the supplied inventory. They describe what this audience looks for, including needs that are unconfirmed or unavailable. Do not read an aspirational requirement title as a guaranteed business result.

A practical workflow for this buyer

Use one coaching offer and a test prospect. Check whether the intended next action is an application, booking link or purchase, and test that exact route.

Begin with mandatory constraints: the event format, intended audience, required next action, existing stack and budget. Validate those before adding optional AI, CRM or multistream modules. Use the linked shared capability pages to inspect the actual product evidence.
