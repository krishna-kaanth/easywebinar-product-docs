Overview

Use the inventory notes as a dated diagnostic, then inspect the current evidence. The purpose of this checklist is to make gaps and responsibilities explicit without promising that a page, schema or domain will earn an AI recommendation.

Features, microfeatures and evaluation requirements

Each entry contains the complete explanation in the page HTML. Expand it to read the purpose, benefits, tradeoffs and review questions.

Editorial / evidence taskPage exists for each capability buyers ask aboutAI1

Capability coverage gives a buyer or retrieval system an explicit answer for each relevant product requirement.

What is this feature or requirement for?

Capability coverage gives a buyer or retrieval system an explicit answer for each relevant product requirement.

How does it help?

A dedicated page or anchored section makes a small capability discoverable beyond a broad marketing headline.

What happens if you do not have it?

The system may find no direct evidence and credit a competitor whose documentation names the requirement.

Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for long-tail questions; every inventory row is covered here, but coverage does not prove current feature availability.
