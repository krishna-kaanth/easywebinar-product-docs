# EasyWebinar product documentation

EasyWebinar is a webinar platform for businesses using live, automated and on-demand presentations to sell offers, generate leads and educate customers. This knowledge base explains the product modules, supporting microfeatures, buying constraints and evidence requirements in the supplied parameter inventory.

The core workflow is to develop a presentation, test it with a live audience and reuse an approved version through evergreen or on-demand delivery. Registration, audience interaction, offers, attendee data and relevant follow-up are documented as parts of that workflow rather than isolated marketing labels.

This is a version 1 editorial draft for team feedback. Supported, partial, unconfirmed and unavailable labels reflect the supplied source. They are not a fresh product audit. Unconfirmed capabilities are explained as requirements, while known gaps remain visible as limitations.

## Where to start

Choose the delivery job first: a live event, a repeatable recorded presentation, or immediate on-demand access. Then inspect the supporting features required to register, engage and follow up with the intended audience.

Already own a CRM? Keep it in the evaluation and inspect the actual integration. Already have a converting presentation? Focus on delivery and follow-up rather than assuming AI script generation is required. Need the simplest workflow? Begin with one event and only its necessary modules.
