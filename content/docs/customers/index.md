## T9 · Customer logos

Status: Partial / plan-gated

Customer logos identify organisations authorised to be represented as product users or customers.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Customer logos identify organisations authorised to be represented as product users or customers.

### How does it help?

A buyer can see whether relevant organisations have a verifiable relationship with the vendor.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Brand familiarity cannot be used as corroboration and the product must be evaluated through other evidence.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for recognition; a logo should not imply a relationship or endorsement beyond what the customer approved.
