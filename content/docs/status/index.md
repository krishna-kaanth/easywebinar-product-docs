A status page and incident history provide an operational record of service availability and disruptions.

### How does it help?

A buyer can inspect current incidents and the vendor's communication during past events.

### What happens if you do not have it?

It depends on individual support replies or anecdotal reports to understand service interruptions.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for operational trust; the beta does not manufacture a status feed or incident history.
