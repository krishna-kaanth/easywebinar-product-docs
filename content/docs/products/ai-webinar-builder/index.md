# Guided AI webinar builder

The guided AI builder prepares draft assets for a webinar campaign, including scripts, page copy, emails and suggested interactions. It reduces first-draft work by keeping the audience, offer and presentation context connected.

The operator still reviews facts and brand language and configures the actual event. This draft does not describe the builder as an agent that publishes an entire fact-checked webinar from one prompt. AI repurposing and AI moderation remain unavailable in the source.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

The guided AI funnel workflow helps a host prepare connected webinar assets from a sequence of inputs.

### How does it help?

The team can draft the script, pages and communications as parts of one campaign instead of unrelated writing tasks.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Preparation requires coordinating multiple drafts and tools independently.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for launch preparation; a quoted ten-to-fifteen-minute drafting estimate is not a guaranteed end-to-end launch time.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.
