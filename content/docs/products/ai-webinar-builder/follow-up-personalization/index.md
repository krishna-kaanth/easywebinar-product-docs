AI follow-up personalisation adapts draft follow-up content to relevant recipient context where supported.

### How does it help?

The team may prepare messages more aligned with a person's webinar activity or audience segment.

### What happens if you do not have it?

It uses manually prepared variants or supported rules-based segmentation.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for relevance; generating personalised copy is different from choosing and sending a message automatically for each attendee.
