### What is this feature or requirement for?

Simulive combines recorded presentation playback with live participation such as a host answering chat.

### How does it help?

It protects the consistency of the presentation while leaving a person available to answer session-specific questions.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A fully live presentation requires repeated delivery; an unattended recording can leave immediate questions unanswered.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It is useful when repeatable production and human responsiveness are both important; prerecorded playback must be described honestly.
