# Automated and evergreen webinars

Automated delivery reuses a recording inside a repeatable webinar workflow. It suits a presentation that needs to reach new people more frequently than a presenter can deliver it live.

Evergreen, just-in-time, simulive and instant-play access are related but distinct modes. Pick the availability and staffing arrangement first; then validate recording, timed interactions, reminders, offers and follow-up for that exact format. The supplied matrix makes automation Growth-and-above.

## U2 · Automated / evergreen webinars

Status: Supported in source

Automated webinars deliver a recorded presentation through a repeatable registration and attendance workflow.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Automated webinars deliver a recorded presentation through a repeatable registration and attendance workflow.

### How does it help?

A successful presentation can serve new prospects repeatedly without requiring the presenter to repeat the full talk.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Every new session requires presenter time, or prospects receive a plain video without the surrounding webinar workflow.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Automation matters when the same sales or education session needs to run more frequently than the team can host live.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.

## U52 · Upload-before-configure for automated events

Status: Partial / plan-gated

Upload-before-configure describes a setup dependency in which the final automated-webinar video is needed before completing event configuration.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Upload-before-configure describes a setup dependency in which the final automated-webinar video is needed before completing event configuration.

### How does it help?

Understanding this dependency lets a team sequence content production and event setup realistically.

### What happens if you do not have it?

If the dependency is unexpected, the team may discover late that it cannot finish automation while the recording is still being edited.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters as a workflow limitation, rather than a selling feature; document which settings can be prepared before video upload.

### Workflow evaluation

Rehearse with the intended cameras, microphones, slides and speaker arrangement. Ask test attendees to inspect the relevant behaviour from the audience side, and check the recording as well as the live presentation.

A successful small rehearsal does not establish large-audience performance. Exact concurrency, latency, session-duration and recovery claims need scoped product documentation or an agreed test.
