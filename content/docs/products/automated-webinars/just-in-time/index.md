### How does it help?

It connects a moment of interest with a near-term viewing opportunity and makes evergreen acquisition campaigns more responsive.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A prospect who arrives between weekly events may lose interest before the next scheduled session.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for always-on campaigns where the time between discovering an offer and seeing the presentation creates avoidable friction.
