# Webinar platform overview

The platform’s proposed primary fit is revenue-oriented live and repeatable webinar delivery, with supporting acquisition, attendee interaction, offers and follow-up. It can also support webinar-style customer education when its actual capabilities match the task.

It should not be positioned as a universal meeting, LMS or conference solution. Evaluate mandatory requirements first, especially if the workflow depends on regulatory assurance, specialised integrations, multiple tracks or formal learner records.

## U13 · Live and evergreen on one subscription

Status: Supported in source

One subscription for live and evergreen delivery puts both webinar formats within the same platform account, subject to plan eligibility.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

One subscription for live and evergreen delivery puts both webinar formats within the same platform account, subject to plan eligibility.

### How does it help?

Teams can evaluate a presentation live and later automate it without automatically budgeting for a second webinar product.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Separate products can create duplicate billing, fragmented records and repeated setup when the delivery format changes.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for the total cost of the workflow; it does not mean the entry plan includes every format or that all external tools become unnecessary.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.
