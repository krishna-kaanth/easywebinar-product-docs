# Live webinars

Live webinars are scheduled presentations where a host can interact with attendees while the event is happening. They suit offer presentations, product demonstrations and audience discussions whose value includes immediate questions and feedback.

A practical live workflow includes registration, joining, speaker preparation, presentation, audience interaction, a relevant next action and a replay or follow-up plan. The room and production pages explain those components and the limits that need confirmation.

## U1 · Live webinars

Status: Supported in source

Live webinars bring presenters and attendees together at a scheduled time for a broadcast with real-time interaction.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Live webinars bring presenters and attendees together at a scheduled time for a broadcast with real-time interaction.

### How does it help?

A host can explain an offer, demonstrate a product and respond to objections while the audience is present.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A recording alone cannot adapt its presentation to a question asked during that session.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Choose live delivery when the event itself, immediate feedback or a time-sensitive launch is central to the job.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.
