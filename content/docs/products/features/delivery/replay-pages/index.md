## U8 · Replay pages and replay scheduling

Status: Supported in source

Replay pages provide a destination for recorded webinar access after the original session, with scheduling where supported.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Replay pages provide a destination for recorded webinar access after the original session, with scheduling where supported.

### How does it help?

People who missed the broadcast can still receive the presentation and attendees can revisit material they did not fully absorb.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The value of the event is limited to people who attend live, or the team must distribute a separate unmanaged video link.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Replay access matters for recovering missed attendance and extending content life; distinguish replay availability from an always-open on-demand library.

## EW44 · Custom replay-page content

Status: Supported in source

Replay content settings configure the supported page around the recorded presentation.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Replay content settings configure the supported page around the recorded presentation.

### How does it help?

The organiser can provide context and a relevant next action for people watching after the session.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The audience uses the default replay presentation or another approved viewing destination.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Replay pages should state that the content is recorded and keep offer dates accurate. Page editing does not establish permanent archive access or a public library.

## EW45 · Replay for no-show registrants

Status: Supported in source

The documented replay setting makes replay access available to eligible people who did not attend the original webinar.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

The documented replay setting makes replay access available to eligible people who did not attend the original webinar.

### How does it help?

A registered person who missed the event can still consume the approved presentation.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The team shares another supported route or loses that viewing opportunity.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Check recipient eligibility, access expiry and the associated message. Replay availability does not guarantee watching or a recovered purchase.
