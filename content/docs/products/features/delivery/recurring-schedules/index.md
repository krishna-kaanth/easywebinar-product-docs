# Recurring schedules

Recurring schedules repeat webinar availability at defined times rather than requiring each date to be recreated separately.

This page explains the requirement in the context of EasyWebinar and preserves the supplied inventory status. Read the evidence section before treating an explanation of buyer value as a promise of current product support.

Recurring schedules sits in the delivery layer of the webinar workflow. Evaluate it together with the event format, the account’s eligible plan and any external provider involved. Its detailed parameter explanation below covers both the benefit and the limits of relying on it.

## U6 · Recurring schedules

Status: Supported in source

Recurring schedules repeat webinar availability at defined times rather than requiring each date to be recreated separately.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Recurring schedules repeat webinar availability at defined times rather than requiring each date to be recreated separately.

### How does it help?

The team can maintain a predictable weekly or regular viewing programme and communicate the next available session.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Staff need to create and maintain individual events, increasing the chance of inconsistent dates and missed reminders.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It is important for repeat demos and onboarding; recurring availability does not itself establish a full multi-session programme product.

## EW18 · Blackout dates

Status: Supported in source

Blackout dates exclude holidays or other unsuitable dates from a supported recurring schedule.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Blackout dates exclude holidays or other unsuitable dates from a supported recurring schedule.

### How does it help?

An evergreen programme can avoid offering sessions when the team or intended audience should not attend.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The organiser manually adjusts session availability and risks overlooking a conflicting date.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Exclusions matter for repeating schedules. Confirm timezone interpretation and whether the setting changes existing bookings or only new session choices.
