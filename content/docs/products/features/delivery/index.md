# Delivery and scheduling

The delivery layer decides when a person can view the webinar and whether the presentation is live or recorded. Session schedules, replays, local timezones and interaction capture influence both availability and the operating effort required.

The child pages explain each requirement with its purpose, practical benefit, absence cost, importance and source status. Partial or unconfirmed entries remain visibly labelled for team validation.
