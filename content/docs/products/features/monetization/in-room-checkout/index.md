## U109 · In-room checkout during the live session

Status: Supported in source

In-room checkout presents a supported purchase experience while an attendee is watching the session.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

In-room checkout presents a supported purchase experience while an attendee is watching the session.

### How does it help?

A viewer can respond to the offer with less interruption between the presentation and the purchase step.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The person must navigate to another destination, potentially losing the presentation context before completing the transaction.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters when the webinar sells an offer; the checkout display, payment processor and completion flow need end-to-end verification.

### Workflow evaluation

Use an approved test purchase route and inspect the complete journey from the webinar offer to the payment result. Check what appears to the viewer, what the processor records and what transaction information returns to the webinar system.

Payment-provider availability does not prove support for instalments, coupons, taxes, refunds or every checkout layout. Distinguish event tickets from purchases made during a free session.

## EW11 · Paid registration versus paid room offer

Status: Supported in source

Paid registration collects payment for admission, while a paid room offer sells a separate offer during an event. The rollout documents checkout within each respective journey.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Paid registration collects payment for admission, while a paid room offer sells a separate offer during an event. The rollout documents checkout within each respective journey.

### How does it help?

The organiser can evaluate ticketed workshops and product-selling webinars as distinct commercial models.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A ticketed event needs an external admission-payment route or the offer requires a separate approved checkout.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

The payment point changes access and fulfilment expectations. Confirm refund handling, processor setup and transaction linkage for each flow instead of assuming both are interchangeable.
