## U120 · QR-code offers for multistream viewers

Status: Supported in source

QR-code offers give external stream viewers a scannable route to the relevant offer.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

QR-code offers give external stream viewers a scannable route to the relevant offer.

### How does it help?

Someone watching on a television or another device can open the action on their phone without typing a long link.

### What happens if you do not have it?

An external audience may see the pitch but lack a convenient way to reach the purchase or signup destination.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for multistream conversion; scanning a code does not automatically identify the viewer or attribute a completed purchase.

### Workflow evaluation

Use an approved test purchase route and inspect the complete journey from the webinar offer to the payment result. Check what appears to the viewer, what the processor records and what transaction information returns to the webinar system.

Payment-provider availability does not prove support for instalments, coupons, taxes, refunds or every checkout layout. Distinguish event tickets from purchases made during a free session.

## U171 · Offers and CTAs reach multistream viewers

Status: Supported in source

Multistream offers and CTAs provide a next-action route for people watching outside the native webinar room.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Multistream offers and CTAs provide a next-action route for people watching outside the native webinar room.

### How does it help?

The external audience can respond to the presentation's offer instead of only watching the broadcast.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The event gains viewers without a clear path for those viewers to register, purchase or take the next step.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for revenue-oriented distribution; document the actual link or QR route and its attribution limits.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.
