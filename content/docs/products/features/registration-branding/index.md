# Registration, pages and branding

Registration turns interest into a usable attendee record. Page content, brand identity, forms, redirects and source data should work as one journey; attractive design alone does not establish qualification or attribution.

The child pages explain each requirement with its purpose, practical benefit, absence cost, importance and source status. Partial or unconfirmed entries remain visibly labelled for team validation.
