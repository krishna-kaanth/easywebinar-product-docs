### How does it help?

The host can explain the next step, reinforce the session details or send the visitor to an appropriate destination.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A generic success screen may leave attendees uncertain about joining information, preparation or what happens next.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for continuity between signup and attendance; document whether the page is hosted, editable or an external redirect.
