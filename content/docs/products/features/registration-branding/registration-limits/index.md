### What is this feature or requirement for?

Registration limits and waitlists control entry when available attendance or ticket allocation is constrained.

### How does it help?

The organiser can manage genuine capacity and explain what happens after places are filled.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Signups may exceed what the event can serve, requiring manual communication or a separate waitlist process.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for limited workshops; registration limits, concurrent room capacity and a waiting-room page are separate concepts.
