# Brand customization

A branded room applies the host's identity to supported parts of the live or recorded webinar environment.

## Product and workflow context

Brand customization sits in the registration branding layer of the webinar workflow. Evaluate it together with the event format, the account’s eligible plan and any external provider involved. Its detailed parameter explanation below covers both the benefit and the limits of relying on it.

The supplied source is an inventory rather than a current implementation manual. Exact configuration steps, UI screenshots and supported limits must be attached during review; this draft does not invent button labels or silently substitute a different feature.

## U31 · Branded room

Status: Supported in source

A branded room applies the host's identity to supported parts of the live or recorded webinar environment.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

A branded room applies the host's identity to supported parts of the live or recorded webinar environment.

### How does it help?

The audience sees visual continuity between the invitation, registration and presentation experience.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The room may look disconnected from the campaign or resemble another vendor's standard environment.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for recognisable customer experiences; room branding and complete removal of platform branding have different scopes.

### Workflow evaluation

Test the attendee journey in the intended browser and device. Follow the invitation, registration and join steps, then try the relevant room interaction using the audience’s normal network conditions. Record where permissions or additional steps appear.

Attendee usability depends on the complete journey, not a single supported screen. Language, captions, account creation and assistive-technology support each require their own evidence.

## U86 · Brand colours, logos, favicon

Status: Supported in source

Brand customisation applies approved colours, logos and related identity elements to supported webinar surfaces.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Brand customisation applies approved colours, logos and related identity elements to supported webinar surfaces.

### How does it help?

Campaigns can match the company's visual identity and make it easier for attendees to recognise the event.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The room and pages use generic styling or differ visibly from the campaign that brought the audience there.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for continuity and trust; clarify which surfaces inherit changes and whether favicon and email identity are included.

### Workflow evaluation

Build a sample registration journey using the intended brand and campaign. Submit a test signup on desktop and mobile, inspect the success step and compare the saved record with the information you expected to collect.

A polished form is only one part of registration. Domain setup, consent, source retention, field mapping and the actual attendee access path each need separate validation.
