### What is this feature or requirement for?

Presenter and co-host biography information introduces the people responsible for the webinar on its registration page.

### How does it help?

Prospects can assess the speaker's relevant experience and understand why the session may be useful.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The visitor sees less speaker context or the organiser adds that information through another supported page element.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Speaker credibility can affect the decision to register. Use accurate, approved biographies rather than implying that adding a bio validates credentials.
