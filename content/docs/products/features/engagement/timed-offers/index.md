## U67 · Timed offers / CTAs

Status: Supported in source

Timed offers and calls to action present a next step at a chosen moment in the webinar.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Timed offers and calls to action present a next step at a chosen moment in the webinar.

### How does it help?

The host can connect an explanation to an offer, signup or other action while that explanation is still relevant.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Attendees may need to search for a link later or act through a disconnected channel after their attention has moved elsewhere.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters when the webinar should lead to an action; a visible CTA does not itself prove an embedded purchase, booking or signup integration.

### Workflow evaluation

Run a short test presentation with a host and an attendee. Trigger the relevant interaction, inspect what both sides see and check any resulting data or follow-up. For recorded sessions, repeat at the relevant playback timestamp.

An interaction visible in the live room may have different controls or limits in automated playback and external streams. Verify the format rather than treating every engagement tool as universally available.
