### What is this feature or requirement for?

Post-event surveys collect structured feedback after attendance.

### How does it help?

The team can learn which questions remain unresolved and whether the session met attendee expectations.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Improvement decisions rely on informal replies or attendance statistics that do not explain the participant's experience.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for iterating programmes; blog advice about surveys does not establish a native survey feature.
