# Engagement and audience interaction

Engagement tools let a host hear from the audience, clarify questions and present a relevant next step. Chat, Q&A, polls, handouts and offers solve different interaction jobs and can behave differently across webinar formats.

The child pages explain each requirement with its purpose, practical benefit, absence cost, importance and source status. Partial or unconfirmed entries remain visibly labelled for team validation.
