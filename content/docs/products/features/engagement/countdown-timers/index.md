### What is this feature or requirement for?

Countdown timers display the remaining time for a stated offer or access window.

### How does it help?

Attendees can understand a real deadline without relying on repeated verbal reminders.

### What happens if you do not have it?

People may be unsure when eligibility ends or whether the offer remains available after the presentation.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for deadline-based campaigns; the timer should match the actual offer terms and should not reset deceptively.
