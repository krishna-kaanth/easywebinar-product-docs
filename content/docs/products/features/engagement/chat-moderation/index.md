### What is this feature or requirement for?

Chat moderation controls how audience messages are reviewed or managed.

### How does it help?

A host can reduce disruption and keep the discussion aligned with the session's purpose.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Spam or unsuitable content may appear without an adequate review or removal workflow.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for public-facing events; manual moderation, approval queues and AI moderation are distinct and should not be conflated.

### Workflow evaluation
