## U127 · Segmentation by engagement and intent

Status: Supported in source

Intent segmentation groups people according to relevant engagement or action signals.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Intent segmentation groups people according to relevant engagement or action signals.

### How does it help?

The sales or marketing team can tailor attention to groups with different observable behaviour.

### What happens if you do not have it?

All leads receive the same priority and communication despite different engagement histories.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for actionable data; a segment based on observed activity should not be described as certainty about a person's intent.

### Workflow evaluation

Use known test contacts with deliberately different attendance and interaction histories. Compare the displayed metric and any export with those actions. Document the metric’s definition, timeframe and whether live and replay data are separate.

Measured webinar activity is not proof of buyer intent, product adoption or CRM closed-won revenue. Attribution claims need the actual transaction connection and a stated model.

## U128 · "First-party intent data" framing

Status: Supported in source

First-party intent data refers to behaviour collected directly through the webinar relationship, such as viewing and interaction.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

First-party intent data refers to behaviour collected directly through the webinar relationship, such as viewing and interaction.

### How does it help?

A business can evaluate interest using its own attendee activity instead of only inferred third-party audience signals.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Sales decisions rely on signup details or external assumptions without evidence of engagement with the actual presentation.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for qualified handoff; define each collected signal and its interpretation without overstating what behaviour proves.
