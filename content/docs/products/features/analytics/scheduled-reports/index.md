## U134 · Scheduled / emailed reports

Status: Unconfirmed

Scheduled reports distribute reporting output automatically at a defined time.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Scheduled reports distribute reporting output automatically at a defined time.

### How does it help?

An agency or manager can receive an update without remembering to open or export each event report.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Reporting delivery requires manual work and may arrive inconsistently across programmes or clients.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for recurring operations; a CSV export is not evidence of scheduled email delivery.

### Workflow evaluation

Use known test contacts with deliberately different attendance and interaction histories. Compare the displayed metric and any export with those actions. Document the metric’s definition, timeframe and whether live and replay data are separate.

Measured webinar activity is not proof of buyer intent, product adoption or CRM closed-won revenue. Attribution claims need the actual transaction connection and a stated model.

### Evidence and review

The source marks this requirement unconfirmed. The explanation describes its meaning and buyer value, not an available EasyWebinar feature. Obtain product evidence before changing the status or publishing an affirmative capability claim.
