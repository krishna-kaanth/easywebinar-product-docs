# Analytics and attendee signals

Analytics describes what registrants and viewers actually did. Define the metric and inspect the underlying data before interpreting it as intent, revenue, pipeline or product adoption.

The child pages explain each requirement with its purpose, practical benefit, absence cost, importance and source status. Partial or unconfirmed entries remain visibly labelled for team validation.

## EW12 · Attendee analytics Beta scope

Status: Partial / plan-gated

The dated analytics rollout measures named attendee actions and playback engagement; its recorded release status is Beta.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

The dated analytics rollout measures named attendee actions and playback engagement; its recorded release status is Beta.

### How does it help?

An organiser can inspect how known participants watched and interacted, then identify places to review in the presentation.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The team uses available basic reports, external measurements or manual observation with clearly stated limitations.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

Beta-labelled functionality needs scoped expectations. Confirm current maturity, definitions, export support and live-versus-replay coverage before upgrading the claim to general availability.

### Workflow evaluation

Use known test contacts with deliberately different attendance and interaction histories. Compare the displayed metric and any export with those actions. Document the metric’s definition, timeframe and whether live and replay data are separate.

Measured webinar activity is not proof of buyer intent, product adoption or CRM closed-won revenue. Attribution claims need the actual transaction connection and a stated model.
