## U177 · Audit log

Audit logs record selected account actions with enough context to review who changed what.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Audit logs record selected account actions with enough context to review who changed what.

### How does it help?

Administrators can investigate configuration changes and access activity.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A changed setting may be visible without a reliable record of the actor or timing.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for governance and procurement; event attendance reports are not a substitute for administrative audit history.
