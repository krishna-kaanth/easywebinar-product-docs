# Team administration

Account administration governs who operates the platform and how responsibilities or client data are separated. Team seats, room moderators, account permissions and sub-accounts should be evaluated as distinct controls.

The child pages explain each requirement with its purpose, practical benefit, absence cost, importance and source status. Partial or unconfirmed entries remain visibly labelled for team validation.
