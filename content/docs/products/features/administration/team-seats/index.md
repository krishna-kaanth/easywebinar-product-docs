### What is this feature or requirement for?

Team seats let more than one authorised person use the platform under the relevant account arrangement.

### How does it help?

People can share production and operational work without treating one operator as the only account user.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The team may need separate accounts or an unsafe shared-login workaround.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for collaborative operations; seat quantity, roles and simultaneous usage need specific plan documentation.
