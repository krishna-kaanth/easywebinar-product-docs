## U181 · SSO / SAML

Status: Partial / plan-gated

SSO and SAML connect supported account sign-in to an organisation's identity provider.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

SSO and SAML connect supported account sign-in to an organisation's identity provider.

### How does it help?

An IT team can evaluate central identity control instead of independent passwords for every authorised operator.

### What happens if you do not have it?

It manages separate accounts and login policy outside its usual identity process.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for enterprise access management; host-account SSO is different from requiring SSO for webinar attendees.
