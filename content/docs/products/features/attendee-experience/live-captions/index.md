## U33 · Live captions

Status: Unconfirmed

Live captions display a text representation of spoken audio while the event is happening.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Live captions display a text representation of spoken audio while the event is happening.

### How does it help?

They can make speech easier to follow for people who cannot hear the audio clearly or who need text support.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Those attendees may miss important content or depend on an external captioning arrangement.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for inclusive attendance and procurement; availability, languages, accuracy and caption correction remain unconfirmed in the inventory.
