## U27 · No attendee account required

Status: Supported in source

Account-free attendance lets a registrant join without creating a separate EasyWebinar attendee account.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Account-free attendance lets a registrant join without creating a separate EasyWebinar attendee account.

### How does it help?

The visitor can focus on the event rather than passwords, account verification and a new software relationship.

### What happens if you do not have it?

A mandatory account introduces additional signup and recovery steps, especially for first-time prospects.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for external marketing webinars; attendee access is distinct from the host account needed to operate the platform.
