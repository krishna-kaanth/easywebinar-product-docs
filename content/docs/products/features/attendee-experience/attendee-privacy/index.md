### What is this feature or requirement for?

Attendee privacy controls determine whether participants can see another attendee's identity or the overall attendee list.

### How does it help?

The host can reduce unnecessary exposure of participants in sensitive or competitor-heavy sessions.

### What happens if you do not have it?

People may be visible to other attendees even when the organiser intended a broadcast-style private audience.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters where attendance itself is sensitive; hiding a list does not prove anonymity of chat, questions or analytics.
