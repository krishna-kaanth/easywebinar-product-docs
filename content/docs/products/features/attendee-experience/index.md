# Attendee experience

The attendee journey runs from opening an invitation to joining, watching and participating. Browser and device support reduce friction, while language, accessibility and privacy requirements determine whether the intended audience can use the experience.

The child pages explain each requirement with its purpose, practical benefit, absence cost, importance and source status. Partial or unconfirmed entries remain visibly labelled for team validation.
