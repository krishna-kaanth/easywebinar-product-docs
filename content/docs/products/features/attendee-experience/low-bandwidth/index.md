### What is this feature or requirement for?

Low-bandwidth performance concerns playback and interaction under constrained or unstable network conditions.

### How does it help?

Attendees with limited connectivity may remain able to follow the session with fewer interruptions.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Buffering, audio loss and delayed interactions can make the webinar unusable for part of the intended audience.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for geographically distributed attendees; acceptable performance must be measured under specified bandwidth and device conditions.
