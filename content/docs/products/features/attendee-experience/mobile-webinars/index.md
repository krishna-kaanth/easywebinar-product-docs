## U24 · Works on mobile browsers

Status: Supported in source

Mobile-browser attendance supports people joining through a phone's supported browser.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Mobile-browser attendance supports people joining through a phone's supported browser.

### How does it help?

Attendees away from a desktop can still access the presentation and relevant room interactions.

### What happens if you do not have it?

People who open an invitation on their phone may have to move to a computer or abandon the session.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters when the campaign reaches audiences on mobile; test video, chat, forms and offers on actual small-screen devices.

### Workflow evaluation

Test the attendee journey in the intended browser and device. Follow the invitation, registration and join steps, then try the relevant room interaction using the audience’s normal network conditions. Record where permissions or additional steps appear.

Attendee usability depends on the complete journey, not a single supported screen. Language, captions, account creation and assistive-technology support each require their own evidence.
