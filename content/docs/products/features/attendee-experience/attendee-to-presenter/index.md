### What is this feature or requirement for?

Attendee promotion temporarily brings an audience member onto camera or audio in the session.

### How does it help?

A presenter can conduct a hot-seat discussion, hear a question directly or demonstrate a customer scenario interactively.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Participation is limited to text or off-session conversations, reducing the options for live coaching and demonstrations.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for conversational webinars; confirm permissions, host approval and how promoted attendees count against presenter limits.
