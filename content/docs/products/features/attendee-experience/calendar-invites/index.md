### What is this feature or requirement for?

Calendar invitations help attendees add the correct webinar time and joining information to their calendar.

### How does it help?

The session can appear alongside other commitments and use the attendee's familiar calendar reminders.

### What happens if you do not have it?

An attendee must manually create an event, potentially entering the wrong timezone or forgetting the joining link.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for professional audiences; an email reminder does not establish support for .ics files or Google Calendar integration.
