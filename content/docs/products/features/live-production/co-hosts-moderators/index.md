## U49 · Co-hosts and moderators

Status: Supported in source

Co-hosts and moderators share operational duties during a webinar.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Co-hosts and moderators share operational duties during a webinar.

### How does it help?

One person can present while another manages audience questions or session administration.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The presenter must switch attention between the talk, chat and room management, increasing the chance of missed questions.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for live events with substantial interaction; specify each role's allowed actions rather than assuming full account-administrator access.

### Workflow evaluation

Rehearse with the intended cameras, microphones, slides and speaker arrangement. Ask test attendees to inspect the relevant behaviour from the audience side, and check the recording as well as the live presentation.

A successful small rehearsal does not establish large-audience performance. Exact concurrency, latency, session-duration and recovery claims need scoped product documentation or an agreed test.
