# On-demand webinars

On-demand delivery lets a person access a recorded presentation without waiting for a scheduled start. It is useful for product research, replay consumption and global customer education.

Instant play is different from a near-term scheduled session and from a browsable library of many recordings. The source confirms on-demand access while leaving a native library and embedded viewing player unresolved.

## U5 · On-demand, instant play

Status: Supported in source

On-demand access lets an attendee begin watching a recorded webinar without waiting for a scheduled broadcast.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

On-demand access lets an attendee begin watching a recorded webinar without waiting for a scheduled broadcast.

### How does it help?

People can evaluate the content during the time they already have available, including outside the host's working hours.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Interested visitors must leave and return later, or depend on a scheduled session they may be unable to attend.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for self-directed demos, global audiences and post-event access; instant play differs from just-in-time scheduling.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.
