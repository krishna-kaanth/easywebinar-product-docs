## U76 · Engagement score per attendee

Status: Supported in source

An attendee engagement score summarises selected participation signals into a per-person indicator.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

An attendee engagement score summarises selected participation signals into a per-person indicator.

### How does it help?

A team can prioritise review of people who engaged more meaningfully than a simple registration record suggests.

### What happens if you do not have it?

All registrants may look equivalent even though some watched, interacted and expressed interest while others never attended.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for sales triage; a score is a configured indicator, not proof of purchase intent or a guarantee that a lead is qualified.

### Workflow evaluation

Run a short test presentation with a host and an attendee. Trigger the relevant interaction, inspect what both sides see and check any resulting data or follow-up. For recorded sessions, repeat at the relevant playback timestamp.

An interaction visible in the live room may have different controls or limits in automated playback and external streams. Verify the format rather than treating every engagement tool as universally available.

## EW05 · EasyCRM lead temperature

Status: Partial / plan-gated

A lead temperature label summarises a supported scoring or prioritisation assessment such as Hot, Warm or Nurture.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

A lead temperature label summarises a supported scoring or prioritisation assessment such as Hot, Warm or Nurture.

### How does it help?

Operators can organise follow-up queues around documented signals instead of treating every registrant as equally ready.

### What happens if you do not have it?

The team reviews activity manually or uses another scoring model.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

A high engagement score does not establish purchasing intent. Confirm inputs, thresholds, refresh timing and plan eligibility before relying on a label.

### Workflow evaluation

Choose a real event timezone and two distinct test attendees. Compare the registration choices, confirmation details, session start, recording playback and permitted interaction. Repeat the test for the exact delivery format you intend to use.

The registration schedule, room behaviour and replay policy are related but not interchangeable. Check supported formats and plan eligibility before assuming a setting applies to live, simulive and on-demand delivery alike.

The source marks this item partial or plan-gated, or its buyer wording is broader than the underlying capability evidence. The supported subset and required plan must be stated explicitly; the draft does not promise the entire requirement.
