### What is this feature or requirement for?

Lead routing assigns relevant leads or follow-up responsibility to supported team destinations.

### How does it help?

A sales team can organise handoff rather than manually deciding who handles every new attendee.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Leads may remain unassigned, be contacted twice or wait for someone to inspect the queue.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for team follow-up; confirm routing conditions, assignment rules and eligible plans.
