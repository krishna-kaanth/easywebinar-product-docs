# EasyCRM

EasyCRM is the built-in CRM module associated with webinar leads and supported follow-up workflows. It is relevant where a team wants to keep a lightweight lead process close to its webinar activity.

If the business already uses a CRM, evaluate the corresponding integration instead of assuming replacement is necessary. Lead scoring, team routing, transferred fields and downstream sales outcomes each require their own scope and plan check.

## U135 · Built-in CRM (EasyCRM)

Status: Supported in source

EasyCRM is the built-in CRM component for managing relevant webinar leads within the platform.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

EasyCRM is the built-in CRM component for managing relevant webinar leads within the platform.

### How does it help?

A small team can keep webinar contacts and supported follow-up activity closer to the event workflow.

### What happens if you do not have it?

It needs an external CRM or a manual process to manage those leads.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters where consolidation is useful; a business with an established CRM should evaluate connection depth rather than assume it must replace that system.

### Workflow evaluation

Prepare a test attendee and the intended receiving application. Run the supported event, inspect the actual trigger and payload, compare transferred fields and check how updates, duplicate records and failures are handled.

A tool name is not an integration specification. Native, middleware, webhook, API, external link and manual workflows have different capabilities, costs and failure modes.
