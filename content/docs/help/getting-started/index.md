# Getting Started

This hub organises getting started within the EasyWebinar documentation. Its child pages explain the relevant workflow, support status and details required to evaluate fit.

Follow the specific capability or tool page for the complete explanation. The review draft includes unresolved requirements so the team can confirm or correct them before customer-facing publication.

## Choose the first workflow

For a first sales webinar, keep the campaign limited to registration, a usable attendee room, a clear offer and a suitable follow-up path. For a repeat demo, decide whether instant on-demand or a scheduled automated session suits the audience.

For customer onboarding, use webinar education language and do not promise learning paths or certificates. For a client programme, resolve brand separation and permissions before duplicating the campaign across accounts.
