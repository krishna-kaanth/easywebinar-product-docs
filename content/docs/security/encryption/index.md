### What is this feature or requirement for?

Encryption in transit protects supported data delivery while information travels over the network.

### How does it help?

A buyer can evaluate the protection applied to communication with the platform.

### What happens if you do not have it?

Unprotected delivery exposes information to avoidable interception risks.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters for baseline data protection; encrypted delivery does not prove encryption at rest, end-to-end encryption or an independent certification.
