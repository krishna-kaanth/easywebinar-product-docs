# Agencies managing client webinar funnels

This workflow is for agencies evaluating repeatable webinar delivery for client campaigns. The aim is a client-specific report or lead handoff, using supported webinar delivery, attendee interaction and the appropriate next-action path.

The requirements below are the complete 25-item buyer set from the supplied inventory. They describe what this audience looks for, including needs that are unconfirmed or unavailable. Do not read an aspirational requirement title as a guaranteed business result.

## A practical workflow for this buyer

Prepare two sample client events with different brands. Verify separation of assets and records, the required permissions and the cost of the intended account arrangement.

Begin with mandatory constraints: the event format, intended audience, required next action, existing stack and budget. Validate those before adding optional AI, CRM or multistream modules. Use the linked shared capability pages to inspect the actual product evidence.
