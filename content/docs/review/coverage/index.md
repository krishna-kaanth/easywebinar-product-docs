## Should related requirements be grouped?

Yes. Shared capability pages should hold related microcontrols and explanatory parameters when they answer one buying question. Buyer-specific phrases should be contextual sections that link to that shared evidence, rather than another duplicate feature page.

This exhaustive beta retains the full architecture so the team can assess every proposed page, including unknown capabilities and review material. The earlier 114-route recommendation remains a smaller production candidate. Approval should decide whether unknown-feature pages belong on the public site, stay internal or merge into a limitations page.

A four-level URL can communicate category and parentage, but depth by itself does not improve understanding or guarantee citation. Useful content, internal links, consistent facts and verified evidence carry the meaning.

## 259 beta routes to 130 proposed production routes

The proposed production structure contains 130 routes, including 7 new shared-page URLs that are not separate routes in this exhaustive beta. The map is a content-migration proposal; no beta route has been removed or redirected.

Keep every explanation during review. For approved merged pages, move the complete microfeature sections, retain their parameter IDs and anchors, then introduce redirects from replaced public routes. Unknown and unavailable requirements stay labelled in limitations; editorial evidence tasks stay internal.

A proposed target URL in the CSV is not automatically a working beta link or a verified product claim. The map explains the editorial decision and makes the necessary new shared pages explicit.
