# Claims requiring confirmation

This beta is useful for internal review precisely because it includes the requirements the product has not confirmed. Do not change an unknown to yes for the sake of a more comprehensive-looking feature list.

The eight major source conflicts concern pricing datasets, automation on the entry plan, Salesforce versus Pardot, SOC 2 readiness versus an audited report, GDPR alignment wording, unsourced uplift statistics, disputed maximum capacity and obsolete third-party pricing.

## Priority questions for product owners

Confirm a single current plan matrix, trial-card requirement, refund and cancellation policy, overage rules and the precise capacities for native live attendance and external streams. The source records Launch as live-only; automation, EasyCRM, checkout and EasyCast begin on Growth in the dated matrix.

Confirm HubSpot fields, sync direction and object/event support; describe Pardot separately from Sales Cloud. Confirm captions, translation, transcripts, accessibility, backstage, failover, roles, sub-accounts, library/player support and actual support commitments.

Verify payment plans, coupons, upsells, tax handling and course fulfilment per connection. Confirm streaming technology wording and measured latency rather than presenting a vendor name as a performance guarantee. Do not publish the unsourced attendance or replay uplift percentages.
