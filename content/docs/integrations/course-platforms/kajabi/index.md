# Kajabi integration

Kajabi is documented here as a unconfirmed connection. The purpose is to connect the relevant webinar workflow with a system the business already uses; it is not a promise of complete two-way sync or every feature offered by that provider.

Evaluate this connection using a real event and a test record or transaction. Availability, authentication, eligible plan, transferred events and receiving data must be confirmed for Kajabi specifically rather than copied from another integration.

## What this connection is for

For Kajabi, start by naming the exact job: transferring a registration, recording an attendance event, sending a follow-up, receiving a purchase event or linking to a next action. Choose only the job supported by the actual connection. This makes the setup testable and prevents a logo from standing in for an integration specification.

The connection can reduce repeated manual entry and preserve continuity with the existing stack when the required action is supported. Without it, the team needs a verified middleware, API, export or manual route; those alternatives have their own operating cost and failure modes.

## Scope the team must verify

Confirm the current Kajabi connector or workflow, authentication method, account permissions, eligible plan, trigger and action list, receiving objects, transferred fields, event timing and supported direction. For a payment tool, also confirm the checkout path and what purchase, cancellation or refund information actually returns.

Test a new record, an existing record and a deliberate failure. Document deduplication, retries, unsupported fields, required external subscriptions and the way the operator can inspect an error. Do not imply certified-partner status or a field count without direct evidence.
