# Support and operational preparation

Support requirements concern how the vendor helps the customer during setup, operation and migration. Channel availability, staffed hours, response commitments and paid services must be verified separately.

This beta supplies planning guidance and source-based support requirements. It does not provide a live support endpoint or promise twenty-four-hour staffing, a dedicated manager or a migration service without current evidence.

## S1 · Support channels (chat, email, phone)

Status: Unconfirmed

Support channels identify how a customer can contact the vendor when a question or incident occurs.

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Support channels identify how a customer can contact the vendor when a question or incident occurs.

### How does it help?

A buyer can determine whether the available contact routes fit its team's working style.

### What happens if you do not have it?

It may expect phone or live chat access that the service does not actually provide.

### Why should it matter to the buyer?

It matters during setup and live events; the source does not confirm the complete channel list or response promise.

### Workflow evaluation

Request the current service description and confirm the channels, hours, eligibility and deliverables relevant to the intended launch. For help material, follow a real supported task and record missing steps.

Written product documentation does not establish staffed support, a response SLA, a migration service or a delivered video tutorial.

### Evidence and review

## S2 · Support hours / 24×7

Status: Unconfirmed

### What is this feature or requirement for?

Support hours define when the vendor's support team is available.
